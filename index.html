The Titan Challenge
You are the new CEO of Titan Consumer Group — one of the world's largest consumer goods companies.

Titan has acquired four ESG brands: companies built on social activism, sustainability, and ethical values. Each came with promises. You must decide how to keep them — or not.

Every decision affects two scores: Investor Confidence and ESG Credibility. If either drops to zero, the board will remove you.

All four brands are real. All decisions are based on real events. You will find out who they are at the end.
